About the Artist

Welcome to the digital realm of Sushmitha, a self-taught artist. Her journey is a fascinating blend of traditional artistic expression and the innovative application of coding skills, which she often weaves into her artwork to create intricate and mesmerizing patterns.

Sushmitha believes that art and technology are not mutually exclusive but rather complementary forces that can unlock new dimensions of creativity. Her portfolio is a testament to this philosophy, showcasing pieces that resonate with spiritual depth, vibrant colors, and the subtle complexity of algorithmic design.
